Connect to DinkusKit replaces pasting the checkout settings and the store key: an Administrator presses Connect on Commerce Settings, the business owner approves at DinkusKit.com, and Commerce gets its own store key, kept encrypted. Commerce connects first; Payments and Ship join the store it makes. Commerce fetches its checkout settings from DinkusKit.com itself, after Connect, on Get checkout settings and on every background check. The Checkout settings and Store key boxes are gone.
