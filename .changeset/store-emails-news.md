Commerce hands order and cart news to the new Store emails plugin and no longer sends email itself. The store's courier reads two new topics from `courier/outbox`. `order.paid` is one item per paid order stored by this version on, with the number, email, delivery address, items with their options and prices, subtotal, discount, shipping and total. `cart.left` is one item per cart left unpaid 10 hours after the shopper gave an email, unless the address is on the stop list, with the tick, the cart link and its stop link. `order.shipped` now also carries the order number, the test mark, and the carrier and tracking number. An item lists at most 50 lines and about 6 KB of them, with `moreLines` counting the rest. The abandoned cart email and its **Commerce settings > Abandoned cart email** choice move to Store emails. Commerce keeps the "Email me with news and offers" tick, the cart link and the stop list, and drops the `email:send` permission. A store that saved the 0.9.0 beta choice keeps loading, and its next settings save drops the choice. A cart a 0.9.0 beta already emailed is never left again.
