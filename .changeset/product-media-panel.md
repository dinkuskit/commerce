A product page gets a second sidebar panel, "Product image & gallery", for the product's picture and up to eight gallery pictures from the Media Library, so they no longer sit at the bottom of the Dinkus Commerce panel. The Dinkus Commerce panel now says "Simple product. Add a choice, like Size, to sell it in more than one version." on a product with no choices. Store settings read the country list in less space; the accepted countries are unchanged.
