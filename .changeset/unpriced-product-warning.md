The Dinkus Commerce panel tells the owner when a product has no Regular price. It says "Add a Regular price to sell it" under the state line, a published page's unpriced product reads "Published: off the shop" instead of "On sale", and a Save that leaves Regular empty answers with the same words as an info notice instead of "Product saved". Saving a choice's price with Regular empty answers the same. What is for sale does not change: a missing Regular price still keeps the product off the shop.
