1. Prepare an EmDash site with Registry sandbox support.
2. Install the stable DinkusKit Commerce listing from your EmDash admin.
3. Connect Commerce first, and approve the store at DinkusKit.com. Then connect Payments to the same store.
4. Follow [Start your store](https://dinkuskit.com/start) through a test order before considering a live launch.

Your site supplies the storefront and its integration. See the [Commerce reference](https://dinkuskit.com/docs/reference/commerce) for the released version's requirements and the [developer integration guide](https://dinkuskit.com/docs/developers/integrate) for the storefront contract.
