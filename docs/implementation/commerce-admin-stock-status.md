# Clerk stock status and out-of-stock listing

The Products page shows In stock, Out of stock, and On backorder when Manage
Stock is off. All three hide when Manage Stock is on. On backorder is that
third stock status, not Allow backorders.

One Save writes Regular, Sale, and stock status. A refused stock write leaves
the stored status unchanged. New products stay In stock until the clerk
changes them.

The Store page is a store-level setting: show out-of-stock products on the
shop, or hide them. Default is show. Hide applies to clerk Out of stock and
Inventory-at-zero Out of stock. On backorder stays visible. Unpriced products
stay hidden. Admin still lists every product.

Manage Stock, Allow backorders, checkout, shipping, taxes, and description
stay later.
