- Installed workerd runtime (`tests/integration/registry-checkout-services.test.mjs`), with a stand-in for DinkusKit.com's trade at a public IP:
  - Each trade is `POST /api/store-passes` on the issuer's origin with `Authorization: Bearer <store key>`, `Content-Type: application/json`, no cookie, and exactly `{ site_id, scope, audience }`. Preparing a checkout trades nothing; starting one trades once for `payments:checkout` and uses that pass for both Payments calls; a status check trades once; opening Orders trades once for `ship:orders`; the background check trades at most once for each.
  - Twelve refusals each cost one trade and reach Payments zero times, and start answers `recoverable-failure`: 401 `invalid_store_key`, 403 `site_mismatch`, `service_not_enabled`, `service_cut_off` and `store_suspended`, 429 `rate_limited`, 503 `signing_unavailable`, a redirect, a 200 without `access_token`, truncated JSON, a coupon-scoped pass and a pass issued in the future. A good answer afterwards checks out.
  - Expired, wrong-scope and wrong-site passes from the trade fail before Payments transport. Replace key stops the old key at once, and pasting the new one, even with a space and a line break around it, checks out again with the bare key as the Bearer token. Without a store key, prepare answers `UNAVAILABLE` and the background check trades and calls nothing.
  - Ship gets each order on its own traded pass with the Payments pass already expired; with Ship turned off, or a Payments pass handed out in its place, nothing calls Ship.
  - Coupons unhooked: the shipped backend contains no `/quotes`, `/redemptions` or `coupons:checkout`. With the coupon service in the settings and a valid, missing, wrong-scope or wrong-site coupon pass, `SAVE10` and `save10` are refused as `COUPON_UNAVAILABLE` (`try-later`) with no cart write, no coupon service call and no Payments call, and the same capability checks out without a coupon, trading only for Payments.
